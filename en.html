Privacy Policy – KinderBudget (תקציב גן)

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · עברית

KinderBudget is an app for keeping a kindergarten's budget: money in, money out, and what is left in each fund. This page explains what data the app keeps, where it goes, and what is not done with it.

In short

  • All data is kept on the phone.
  • The app has no server of its own, and no account with the developer is needed.
  • The developer does not receive, see or keep any data from the app.
  • There are no ads, and no tracking or usage analytics by the developer.
  • Backups and reports go to Google Drive only if the user's Google account is connected, and only to that account's Google Drive.

What is kept on the phone

  • Entries: amount, date, fund, merchant or source, business ID, invoice number and note.
  • Receipt photos and PDF files – only when chosen to be kept with an entry.
  • Merchant names as corrected, and the app's settings.
  • The address of the connected Google account, if one is connected – to show which account it is.

A receipt may carry personal details printed on it, such as the last digits of the payment card used. These are not kept as part of the entry, but they appear in the photo itself if it is kept. From a receipt the app reads only the merchant, business ID, invoice number, date and amount. A photo chosen to be kept is kept as taken.

Camera and files

The camera is used only to photograph receipts. Images, PDF files and CSV files are read only when chosen, or when shared to the app from another app.

Reading receipts

Receipts are read on the phone itself. The images and the text read from them are not sent anywhere.

Text recognition uses Google's ML Kit (through Google Play services) and a Hebrew reading model included in the app. According to Google, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage data: device details (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier not intended to identify a user or device, performance metrics, the recognition settings and error codes – not the images and not the text. Google states that it does not transfer this data to third parties. Google's full disclosure.

Google Drive (optional)

If a Google account is connected, the app saves backups, and reports created as Google Sheets, in a KinderBudget folder in that account's Google Drive. Its permission covers only files the app itself created: the rest of the Google Drive is not accessible to it. Requests go from the phone directly to Google on behalf of the connected account, and the developer has no access to them or to the files. The account can be disconnected at any time in the settings, which revokes the access. The automatic backup includes the entries and their receipt photos. It can be set to save the entries only (without the photos), or turned off altogether. Files in Google Drive are covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Copies on the phone

Once a week the app saves a backup copy in Download/KinderBudget (or a folder chosen instead), keeping the last five. By default the copy includes the entries and their photos. It can be set to save the entries only (without the photos). PDF, Excel and CSV reports are created only when asked for, and saved or shared as chosen.

Temporary files and the trash

Photos taken during a scan are kept temporarily in the app's cache memory, and removed automatically the first time the app is opened after they are 30 days old.

A deleted entry stays in the trash for 30 days, then is removed for good the same way. The trash can also be emptied by hand.

Sharing

The data is not sold, and is not passed to the developer or to any third party. It leaves the phone only to the connected account's Google Drive, or wherever the user chooses to share it.

Deletion

  • The data stays on the phone until it is deleted or the app is uninstalled.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its data.
  • Backups in the Download folder stay until they are deleted.
  • Files in Google Drive stay until they are deleted.
  • The trash can be emptied by hand at any time.

Children

The app is meant for kindergarten staff and does not ask for any information about children. There is no need to enter children's personal details in it.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published on this page with the date of the update. The page's full history is kept on GitHub.
